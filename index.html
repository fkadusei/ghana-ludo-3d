      Ludo Rules Guide

      Goal

      Move all four of your pawns from base to the center home by rolling the dice and moving clockwise.

      Starting

      • Click “Decide Starter” to pick the first player.
      • You must roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of base.
      • Rolling a 6 grants an extra turn.

      Movement

      • Move clockwise along the outer track by the rolled number.
      • After completing a full loop, enter your colored home lane.
      • Pawns must count exact steps to reach home.

      Captures & Safe Squares

      • Landing on a lone opponent captures it and sends it back to base.
      • Safe squares are the colored starts and the star squares eight steps after them.
      • Two pawns of one color on the same square form a blockade that others cannot pass.

      Backward Capture

      • Backward moves are allowed only when they capture a single opponent.
      • If both forward and backward moves are legal, you choose the direction.

      Controls

      • Drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. On touch: one finger orbits, two fingers pinch/twist, double-tap empty space to reset the view.
      • Click the dice, the Roll button or press Space to roll.
      • Click or tap a glowing pawn to move it (taps snap to the nearest movable pawn).

      Play vs Computer

      • Tap Play vs Computer, pick your color, 1–3 opponents and Easy / Normal / Hard.
      • Solo games are saved automatically; reload the page to resume.

      Online play

      • Tap Play Online, then Create Room and share the 5-letter code or the invite link.
      • The host picks who plays: friends take open seats, and any seat can be set to a Computer player.
      • If someone disconnects, the computer covers for them until they rejoin with the same code.
      • Tap React to send an emoji or a quick phrase that pops up over your yard.
      • Connections are peer-to-peer; if a strict network blocks them, try another network.

      Winning

      The first player to move all four pawns into the center wins.
